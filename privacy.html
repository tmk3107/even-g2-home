Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

What the app accesses

Even G2 Home requests read-only access to Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly). It reads event titles, times, locations and descriptions of the signed-in user's calendars, only to display them on that user's own Even Realities G2 glasses.

How the data is used and stored

  • Calendar data is used only to display the user's own schedule. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The OAuth refresh token, and any calendar data the app keeps (for example a short-term cache to make the display faster), are stored only on infrastructure controlled by the user: the user's own computer or the user's own cloud account (Amazon Web Services). No one else operates or has access to that infrastructure.
  • The app never writes to, modifies or deletes calendar data.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

This app is used only by its developer, so no public contact channel is provided.
